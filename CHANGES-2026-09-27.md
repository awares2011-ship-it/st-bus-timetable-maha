# Session changes — 2026-09-27

## Data: all 31 MSRTC divisions, all 36 districts, all 358 talukas

- **Source:** the 31 official division timetable PDFs published at
  `msrtc.maharashtra.gov.in/GeneralPages/Timetabel.aspx` (downloaded to
  `data/msrtc/pdf/`). They're scanned images, so each page was read and
  transcribed by hand into `data/msrtc/source/pages/<division>-pNN.json`
  (compact `timetable-page` format — see `scraper/parsers/json-fixture-parser.ts`).
- **Coverage per page** is in `data/msrtc/coverage.json` (done / illegible /
  partial / arrivals-only / seasonal-skipped). Every page note records what
  was left out and why. Nothing is guessed: unreadable cells,
  "every 30 min" frequency notes and ambiguous through-services are omitted.
- **Result:** 30,710 departures · 6,602 routes · 2,458 places · 31/31
  divisions with schedules (was 3,945 trips in 15 divisions).
  305 of 359 taluka HQs have buses (140 with their own departure board).
- **Geography:** `scripts/geography/maharashtra_geography.py` →
  `data/msrtc/reference/geography.json` (31 divisions with region + official
  PDF link, 36 districts, 359 talukas with Marathi names and HQ towns);
  `apply_geography.py` merges it into the reference stops and writes
  `districts.json`. Same-name towns are disambiguated
  (Karjat, Ashti, Kalamb, Malegaon, Malkapur…).
- **Pipeline:** division-aware name resolution (`StopResolver.resolve(name, divisionHint)`),
  new villages are created inside the source division instead of dropped,
  `unverified` fixtures flagged per trip, published JSON is minified.

## Follow-up: interconnected search, Marathi default, multicolour UI

### Interconnected routes (e.g. Pune → Satara)
`src/search/journeyPlanner.ts` + `src/hooks/useJourney.ts`, used by `/search`.
A search now returns **every bus of the day** between two places:
- **All stands of a city** (`data/msrtc/reference/cities.json`): Pune = Swargate,
  Shivajinagar, Station, Vallabhnagar, Pimpri-Chinchwad; Mumbai, Thane,
  Kolhapur, Solapur, Nanded likewise. Each row shows which stand.
- **Direct** — the stand's board lists the destination (exact time).
- **Going beyond** — board lists a farther town and the destination lies on
  that corridor (e.g. Pune → Kolhapur buses for Satara). Exact departure;
  the stop is inferred from geography and labelled so.
- **Passing through** — buses from other depots (Mumbai, Thane, Borivali,
  Ahilyanagar…) whose route passes the origin then the destination; the time
  at the origin is an estimate (≈, 40 km/h × 1.25 road factor).
- **One change** — when through buses are scarce, hubs with times for both legs.
- Filters by kind and stand, date picker, "hide departed", return trip, next
  bus highlighted.

Pune → Satara: 191 buses on a weekday (30 direct incl. Swargate non-stop
every 30 min, 100 going beyond, 61 passing through).

### Coordinates
`scripts/geography/geocode_stops.py` (GeoNames IN, CC BY 4.0) and
`geocode_wiki.py` (Wikipedia coordinates) → `data/msrtc/reference/stop-coords.json`.
358/359 taluka HQs and ~1,330 of 2,520 places geocoded; matches must fall in
the stop's district / near its division, otherwise left blank (never guessed).
Places without coordinates still work for direct results; they just can't be
used for corridor inference.

## Search accuracy fixes (after "Pune → Shevgaon shows wrong buses")

Audit: `npx tsx scripts/debug/audit.ts` → `data/.tmp/audit.md` (bad coordinates,
far route origins, same-name mix-ups, and every district-HQ pair searched).
Explain one search: `npx tsx scripts/debug/plan.ts <fromId> <toId>`.
Suspicious search results went 976 → 1 (that one is a genuine printed route).

- **Place matching:** a village name on a board only matches a village in the
  same division; a Marathi-only match must agree with the English spelling
  (Konkan "Devli" ≠ Wardha "Deoli"); all same-named candidates are tried
  (same division → any town). Aliases that are another taluka town's real
  name are dropped ("Khed" was hijacked by Rajgurunagar).
- **Plausibility pass:** a far cross-division destination switches to a
  nearer same-named place when one exists (Solapur's "Malegaon" → near
  Baramati). Unique far destinations (Pandharpur, Akkalkot…) are kept.
- **Via stops:** a via far off the route is dropped (town) or made a local
  village ("Pimpri" on Basmat–Jintur is not Pimpri-Chinchwad).
- **Geometry:** only district HQs / city stands may bend a route (≤ 10 %
  detour); any other town must be within ~7 % of the route length of the
  straight line. Alibag and Palghar are not through-hubs. No inference for
  trips < 25 km or within one city; "passing" estimates only for buses that
  started ≤ 250 km earlier.
- **Coordinates:** division anchors verified (Chandrapur city is "Chānda" in
  GeoNames — the old anchor was 115 km off); far big-city matches only for
  places outside Maharashtra. 358/359 taluka HQs placed.
- **Mumbai group** narrowed to Mumbai Central / Dadar / Parel.
- **Through buses:** new `throughFrom` / per-row origin in page fixtures;
  routes carry a `timingPoint` (where the printed time applies). The planner
  shifts times from that point. Shirur board (Pune p6) transcribed: Pune →
  Shevgaon ×15/day, Beed ×15, Nanded, Parbhani, Jalgaon… plus buses towards
  Pune from Hingoli, Yavatmal, Buldhana, etc.
