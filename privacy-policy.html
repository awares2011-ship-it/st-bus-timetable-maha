Privacy Policy for ST Bus Timetable Maha

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ST Bus Timetable Maha ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Source

All bus timetable data, routes, depot information, and schedules displayed in this application are sourced from the official Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation (MSRTC) website. We do not claim ownership of this data.

2.2 Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information from users. The app does not require user registration, login, or any form of personal data submission.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

When you use the app, certain information may be automatically collected, including:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features accessed, search queries, viewing patterns
  • Location Data: Approximate location (if you grant permission) to show nearby depots and routes

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the app. AdMob may collect and use certain information for advertising purposes, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • App usage information
  • Ad interaction data

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization).

3.2 Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how users interact with the app. These services collect anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the app experience.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Provide and maintain the app functionality
  • Display relevant bus timetables and route information
  • Show location-based features (with your permission)
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Analyze usage patterns and fix bugs

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your information to third parties. Information may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • Third-Party Service Providers: With services like Google AdMob that help operate our app
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale, or acquisition

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require any registration or personal data submission.

8. Location Permissions

The app may request access to your device's location to provide features such as:

  • Finding nearby bus depots
  • Showing relevant routes based on your location

Location access is optional, and you can deny or revoke this permission at any time through your device settings. The app will continue to function without location access.

9. Data Retention

Since we do not collect or store personal user data on our servers, there is no data retention policy for personal information. Timetable data is updated periodically from the MSRTC official source.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review any information we may have collected
  • Request deletion of your data (though we don't store personal data)
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Disable location permissions
  • Uninstall the app at any time

11. Disclaimer

This app is not officially affiliated with Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation (MSRTC). All timetable information is sourced from the official MSRTC website and is provided "as is" without any warranties. We are not responsible for any inaccuracies, changes, or delays in the timetable data.

Users are advised to verify critical travel information with official MSRTC sources before making travel plans.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
